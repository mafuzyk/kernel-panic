# Code of Conduct

KERNEL PANIC is a small, open-source project and should be a welcoming place for contributors with different levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate clearly, constructively, and in good faith;
- focus criticism on code, design, behavior, and evidence rather than people;
- respect different backgrounds, accessibility needs, and working styles;
- give maintainers enough context to reproduce a problem;
- accept that a maintainer may defer or reject a change for scope, safety, or quality reasons.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized or hateful conduct, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission.

If a serious incident occurs, contact the project maintainer privately with the relevant links, dates, and evidence. Do not escalate a conflict by publishing personal information or by attempting to retaliate. Maintainers may remove content, pause participation, or take other reasonable action to protect the project and its community.
